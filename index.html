2000 to 2010

A decade, one year at a time.

Scroll down and the line fills in as you move forward through the decade. Click a year to open its months.

The Estrada impeachment trial dominates the news, while noontime TV keeps households company through it.

EDSA II ousts Estrada. Teleseryes lean harder into rags-to-riches plots, giving ordinary families a story to hold onto.

OPM acts like Rivermaya and Kamikazee anchor a distinctly local sound against Western radio.

The Oakwood mutiny shakes Manila. Coup anxiety becomes background noise for the rest of the decade.

Texting is now a daily habit, and the country earns its reputation as the texting capital of the world.

The Hello Garci wiretap scandal breaks, and public trust in institutions takes another hit.

Friendster spreads through internet cafes as a state of emergency is declared amid coup rumors.

Gloc-9 and Pinoy hip-hop hit mainstream radio, rapping about poverty and family like it's lived-in, not performed.

A global financial crunch makes OFW remittances more visible, and more essential, than ever.

Typhoon Ondoy floods Metro Manila. A shared disaster becomes a shared memory across the country.

The country holds its first automated national elections, changing how Filipinos vote and how fast results arrive.

Affects, senses, and feelings

Things nobody thought twice about.

The click of a T9 keypad, typing out a message one careful letter at a time. The taste of chocolate from a box someone mailed home from abroad. A classmate's messy handwriting filling the last page of a yearbook. None of it seemed like much back then.

Looking back, these small, unremarkable things carried something real, a kind of closeness that's easy to miss if you're only paying attention to the headlines.

Space and places

Where the decade actually happened.

A lot of it happened outside the home, in ordinary shared spaces that brought people together without anyone planning it that way.

Tiangge and sari-sari stores

Kept whole neighborhoods connected through nothing more than errands and small talk.

Computer shops and malls

Turned into hubs for socializing and catching up on whatever trend was moving that month.

Field trips

Gave students a version of the world outside the classroom, one bus ride at a time.

Technology

One text, one song, one click at a time.

This was the decade the country shifted from an analog life to a digital one, faster than most people realized while it was happening.
